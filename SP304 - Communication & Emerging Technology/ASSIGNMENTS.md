## Notes
- Additional instructions for all assignments are provided via Canvas and in class.
- **Quizzes:** multiple choice / true-false / short answer, delivered asynchronously on Canvas.
- **Individual Analysis Paper:** explore one topic of interest in depth; choice of several options.
- **Emerging Technology Workshop:** group project — design, execute, and assess an interactive workshop on a communication/emerging-tech issue; groups present an evaluation during the final week of class.
- **Research and Reflection Exercises:** complete an activity, then respond to reflection questions.
- **Peer & Group Feedback:** constructive feedback to peers/group members at multiple points in the semester.
- **Participation and Reflection Activities:** reflection prompts, media examples, self-assessments, collaborative documents, discussion boards, etc.
- Deadlines are generally the start of class on the assigned date unless otherwise specified; late work loses 10%/day unless arranged in advance. Some time-sensitive group activities cannot be submitted late at all.
